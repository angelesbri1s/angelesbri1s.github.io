Ángeles Briones

[ Hola hola ]

Information designer, researcher, and Ph.D. in Design from the Politecnico di Milano. The Alternative Narratives Visualization Archive collects some of the case studies used in her doctoral research Disclose to tell. A data design framework for alternative narratives.

For ten years, she worked as a postdoctoral research fellow at the DensityDesign Research Lab. Some projects are Operación Limpieza Nicaragua. Junio-Julio 2018, and The GAFAM Empire and Archive and the City: Serendipity Between Memory and Urban Space. Architecture Biennale di Venezia. More projects will be part of this ongoing website.

She currently teaches at the Politecnico di Milano School of Design, NABA, and BAUER, and works as a freelance designer and independent researcher. Since 2018 she is part of the Public Data Lab, an international network of researchers working on the development of new formats for the creation and use of public data.

Her work sits at the intersection of data visualization, digital methods, and activism, exploring how data can articulate alternative narratives and open spaces for critical reflection on the social, particularly in academic and Human Rights contexts.
